# ADR 0004: The Mythos layer as built (Milestone 3b)

Status: accepted (implementation). Builds on ADR 0002 (extension points) and ADR 0003 (story data).

## Context
The horror layer ships with 1.0, is player-tunable (off / mild / full, default full) and must leave the cozy game complete at "off". Nothing in the core assemblies may depend on it or contain cult content.

## Decisions
1. **Everything lives in `Farm.Mythos` and data.** The core gained only generic hooks: `INpcScheduleSource` (extra schedule entries for an NPC), `IJournalPage` (extra journal sections), `StoryContent` `setEntries` (append entries to an existing dialogue set), a Forest/Woods route edge pair, and `GameSession.HorrorLevel` / `HorrorLevelOverride` (tests). The Woods scene and the layer's pack ship in the build, but every way in is gated by the flag `woods.open`, which only the layer sets.
2. **Level scaling.** `MythosLevel.Scale` is 0, 0.5, 1. Every hook reads the level live. At level 0 no hook changes state, no condition atom passes, the Woods stay closed, and the story data (all gated `horror:1` or `horror:2`) is inert. The saved state (variables, flags, module data) is kept when the level is turned down, so turning it back up resumes.
3. **Wakefulness** is the permille variable `mythos.wakefulness`; the step (0..20) is mirrored in `mythos.step` for conditions. It rises by the calendar overnight (25% per season exactly, `WakefulnessModel`), each successful ritual lowers it 30-40%. A step change publishes `WakefulnessStepChanged`, which gives the atmosphere layer `mythos.dread` a new tint and the journal a new lore line. There is no HUD meter. At 1000 the event `mythos_ending_awakening` runs and the game ends (the fiery ending).
4. **Rituals** run on day 4 of each season at 22:00 in the Woods clearing. Offerings (2/3/4/5) and their Keepers are planned at season start (`RitualDirector.PlanSeason`, deterministic from the world seed); each is a real object with probability 25% (consumed through the world-object sources), otherwise a village item. A ritual advances with the clock (`MinuteChanged`) and is completed at night fall even when the player sleeps. It fails if any Keeper is sick or away, or an offering was taken or is missing. The player can interfere from the first summer (flag `mythos.interference`) by taking an offering from the altar, or by making a Keeper ill through Hazel. A failed ritual lowers nothing; with all five Keepers available the layer never wakes the god by itself (a test simulates six years of an ignoring player).
5. **The Keepers** are Tilda, Marcus, Dr. Penn (Odalys, the leader), Dorian and Wren; Hazel works against them. Allegiance is a table in `MythosCast`, not a field on the NPC. Their ritual-night and unavailable schedules come from `MythosScheduleSource` (built from each Keeper's own workday, so shops still open); clue lines are appended to `npc.<id>.talk` through `setEntries` and gated by `keeper:<npc>` and `var:lore`.
6. **Dread** is the variable `dread` (0..100), moved by story effects, dreams and the wakefulness step's floor, and decays each morning. It scales luck (up to -0.6), the odds of fog and storms, bad/good event weights and friendship decay (`DreadModel`). At mild all factors are halved. Horror crops (`nightbloom`, `hollowroot`, `ashfruit`) have grow conditions `var:dread>=20/40/60`; ordinary mature crops can turn into three strange (and sellable) cousins at up to 5% a night (`MythosMutation`), never in the greenhouse, never at level 0.
7. **Endings:** awakened (the god wakes), sealed (three relics at the altar with enough lore), joined (initiated with standing 20), ignored (year 3 without either). Ending flags are `ending.*`; the game continues after sealed, joined and ignored.
8. **Presentation** is data-light: a 20-step ladder of tint, extra fog odds and a dread floor (`WakefulnessLadder`), a blood moon from step 10 at full intensity, an extra atmosphere layer in the Woods, and altar lights that dissolve instead of any explicit imagery. Text distortion is not implemented (not needed for the ladder; a filter hook exists if wanted).

## Consequences
- Content pack and Woods scene are generated by `MythosGenerator` and `MapBuilder.BuildWoods`; story and strings are authored in `Resources/Mythos/mythos_story.json` and `en.json`.
- `GameDatabase.Merge` is idempotent per pack instance (the database asset outlives sessions in the Editor).
- Art, audio and the ladder's sounds are placeholders. Balance (dread numbers, mutation rate) has been verified by simulation only, not by a person.
