## Short description (Steam: 200-300 characters, plain text; this one is 271)
A cozy pixel-art farming sim with a cosmic-horror secret. Till, plant and harvest through the seasons, fish, mine and befriend your neighbors, then decide whether to stop, aid or join the midnight rituals in the woods. Horror intensity is yours to set: full, mild or off.

Alternatives:
- Grow crops, raise animals, fish, mine and win over a tight-knit village in this cozy pixel-art farming sim. But under the new moon, someone is gathering in the woods. Follow the clues, disrupt the rituals or join them, and tune the horror from full to off. (Animals are in the game now, so this one is accurate.)
- A cozy farming life sim where something ancient sleeps in the woods. Plant and harvest across four seasons, fish, mine, craft and make friends, or follow the villagers into the woods by night and choose whether to help or sabotage their rituals. Horror can be turned down or off.

A cozy farming life sim with a secret buried in the woods.

Inherit a neglected farm on the edge of Wetherell, a quiet New England village where everyone knows everyone. Clear the land, grow crops through the seasons, fish the rivers, delve into the mine and earn a place among your new neighbors. It is a warm, unhurried life.

But on the darkest night of every new moon, some of those neighbors slip away into Harrow Wood. What they do there, and why, is up to you to discover. Help them, stop them, join them, or simply keep farming.

[h2]A cozy farming game first[/h2]
Every day brings a handful of small, satisfying choices. Till, plant, water and harvest crops across four 28-day seasons, then sell your goods, upgrade your tools and turn a weed-choked plot into a thriving homestead. Nothing punishes you harshly, the game saves every night, and the whole farming and friendship experience is complete on its own.

[h2]A village that lives without you[/h2]
Twelve villagers keep their own schedules by day and by night. Learn what each of them loves, share gifts and conversation, unlock heart events and celebrate a festival every season. Help restore the old Community Hall, room by room, and watch the village come back to life around you. Relationships are optional, and all of them are all-ages.

[h2]Something sleeps in Harrow Wood[/h2]
Beneath the warmth, small things start to feel wrong. Villagers keep odd hours. Strange crops sprout that no shop will sell. The weather turns, and the fog does not always lift. Overhear conversations, follow the clues, and track the villagers into the woods to see what really happens at the altar. Your choices shape the fate of Wetherell, and lead to very different endings.

## Content notes (honest horror marketing)
Atmospheric and psychological dread, folk-horror and cosmic unease: a secretive cult, rituals (offerings dissolve into light; no harm to people or animals is shown), creeping unease, strange weather and crops, an end-of-the-world ending. No gore, no sexual content, no real-world religion. Relationships are all-ages. The intensity setting and a content-notes screen are in Options, and the store page must say so.

## Steam content survey (draft answers; the owner submits them)

- Frightening or disturbing content: yes, optional and player-tunable (see above).
