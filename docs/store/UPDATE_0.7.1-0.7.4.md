Wetherell Farm Saga: updates 0.7.1 to 0.7.4

NEW
- Run: hold Shift (or the left trigger on a pad) to move 1.75 times faster. It costs a little energy, and you walk when it runs out. You can rebind it in Options.
- Books: borrow one from the library and read it from your item bar. Villagers like to talk about what you have read.
- "How many?": buying, selling and shipping now open a window with a number box and -All, -5, -1, +1, +5 and +All buttons. A single item is one click.
- Recipes show how much energy and health each dish restores.
- Mouse and controller sensitivity sliders, in Options under Keyboard and mouse and under Controller. They take effect at once.
- Doors inside the house open and close as you pass, and doorways are wider.
- More to discover: new scenes between villagers, something new to restore in the Community Hall, and 144 new lines of village talk, including reactions to what you have been up to.

CHANGES
- The village roads now wind like the farm path, and are dirt instead of straight cobbles (the square stays cobbled).
- The pickaxe and axe no longer pick up chests, machines or sprinklers. Use the builder's mallet.
- Warnings, such as the torch warning, pause the game until you dismiss them.
- The shipping window cursor follows the grid, and a pad steps through every slot.
- Fishing shows the perfect-catch zone.
- Hearts gained are announced after a chat, not underneath it.
- Tidier villager dialogue, and requests that ask you to learn a skill rather than hand over a villager's own stock.
- New pictures for a handful of small items and objects.

FIXES
- Village buildings showed a missing-text label when you pointed at them.
- The hotbar's row buttons no longer cover the last slot of the bar.
