Wetherell Farm Saga: update 0.7.5

NEW
- A real village square: a cobbled plaza where the road crosses the lane, with a large fountain in the middle that spits water, a ring of paving stones round it, benches, lamp posts and the clock tower. The roads widen and blend into the cobbles instead of meeting them at hard corners. The festivals (the dance, the feast tables and the judging table) moved to fit it.
- Driftwood on the beach: a different number of pieces washes up every day between the water and the fish stall. Cut it with an axe for wood. There are rocks to break with the pickaxe too, and everything on the beach has a name when you point at it.
- Options has been rebuilt for controller and keyboard players. A list of categories on the left, one short page on the right. LB and RB (or Q and E) change the page from anywhere, left and right change a slider, a choice or a toggle, and the cursor never wanders off the page. The controller sensitivity is two presses of LB from opening the screen. Waiting for a key or a controller button now gives up after five seconds, so a pad player is never stuck.
- New furniture icons for the armchair, bench, chair, clock, dining table, lamp, painting, rug and vase.
- Horror layer (if you have it on): the deep mine, from the floor of the old chest down, is dressed with veins, urns, columns and cracked floor, and at full intensity with eyes and statues, more of it the deeper you go and the more afraid you are. At full intensity tentacles rise round the altar on a ritual night.

CHANGES
- Trees in the village stand in copses instead of diagonal rows.
- Every trail on every map is now the brown of the dirt trail, not the pale sand colour.
- The torch and the village street lamps light twice as far.
- The little flower boxes, planters, barrels, crates and wheelbarrow in the village are gone: they were only things to walk round. The brown patches on the green are grass.
- The notice board now stands by the pond, and the clock tower is south-east of the square.

FIXES
- The pebbles and driftwood drawn on the beach sand could not be picked up and had no names. They are real now (see above).
