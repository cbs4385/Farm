## About the game
You've inherited a run-down farm on the edge of Wetherell, a quiet New England village where everybody knows your name by the end of the first week. Clear the fields, rebuild the farmhouse, and settle into the rhythm of the seasons: befriend twelve villagers, each with their own story, fish the pond and the beach, work the mine, cook, forage, and make the farm unmistakably yours.

But Wetherell keeps an old secret. In the woods beyond the village, something vast sleeps, and the town has its ways of keeping it that way. An optional folk-horror story runs underneath the cozy game, and you decide how much of it you want: turn it off entirely, let it whisper at the edges, or follow it all the way down.

The cozy game is whole on its own. The horror is there if you go looking.

## Key features
- Restore your farm across four full seasons: crops, animals, orchards, a greenhouse, and a farmhouse that becomes a home. Move the coop, barn and greenhouse wherever suits you.
- Twelve villagers to befriend, each with their own routines, troubles, and stories
- Fish, mine, forage, and cook. Take a torch down the dark mine, and ride the elevator back out when the day runs short.
- A gentle start: a skippable tip card walks you through your first days, and your controller's own button names appear wherever the game tells you what to press.
- An optional folk-horror layer woven through the village: tune it Off, Mild, or Full
- Hand-crafted pixel art, with villagers who walk, work and swing their tools, and an atmosphere that shifts with the seasons and the mood

A greenhouse with its own look, a first-days tip card, hover names for furniture, saving feedback, deleting saves, a mail indicator, quest markers over villagers, a rain of coins when gold comes and goes, a darker mine with craftable torches and an elevator exit, and a redrawn mine entrance.

## FAQ
**When does it release?** Soon. Playtesters hear everything first.

**What platforms?** Windows, Linux and macOS, via Steam.

**How much will it cost?** To be announced. Testers will know before anyone else.

**Is there an NDA?** No. Just don't share Steam keys or game files: keys are per-person.

**Where do I report bugs?** The in-game reporter (pause menu) packages your logs and emails them to me directly. Or post in the Discord's #bug-reports channel.

**I'm not into horror. Is this game for me?** Yes. The horror layer can be switched off entirely, and the farming game underneath is complete without it.
