# Narrative bible: the six Enhanced villagers (Marcus, Odalys, Felix, Dorian, Elara, Ione)

Status: **accepted under the owner's standing pre-approval of agent recommendations (2026-10-04)**; the owner has not read it. The template and village facts are in `BIBLE.md`; Wren, Hazel, Bram (approved) and Tilda, Juno, Piper (`BIBLE_WAVE2.md`, approved) are written at Full depth. These six launch at Enhanced depth (about 75 talk lines, three heart events, one quest, 15 barks, a social profile, gift reactions) and are brought to Full in the free updates (T-150). Everything was checked against the shipped roster data, names, tastes, schedules and existing lines, and `mythos/LORE.md`.

Pronouns: the shipped data has none; this bible uses the pronouns of the art and existing lines (Marcus, Felix and Dorian he; Odalys, Elara and Ione she). Lines that talk about them use those pronouns; nobody guesses the player's.

## 1. Marcus Dell (carpenter)

**Role and place.** The village carpenter: barns, coops, greenhouses, shelves. Closed Wednesdays (he sits on a bench with Bram). Dorian lodges with him. Birthday Summer 9. Not romanceable.
**Public face.** Solid, patient, unhurried. Speaks like a man laying a floor.
**Real self.** Carries his father's habit of measuring three times because he is afraid of building something that will not last. The Community Hall's roof has been "nearly fixed" for years, and he knows exactly how long.
**Voice rules.** Measured, short declaratives (up to 16 words), craft proverbs (grain, joint, plumb), no exclamation marks. Never rushes anyone; never criticizes another's work.
**Humour type.** Patient understatement.
**Signature bit: *Measure twice*.** He measures things out loud, twice, and says the third time under his breath ("Measure once. Twice. ...Three times, my father said."); the hall roof is always "nearly" done. Catchphrase: **"Measure twice."** Sound: two pencil taps.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: the hall roof finished; a building that outlives him. Fears: a joint that gives way.
**Relationships.** *Bram:* the bench, the silence, the best friendship in the village. *Dorian:* his lodger; they share a house and about twelve words a week. *Tilda:* buys nails by the pound and settles in kindnesses. *Odalys:* he trusts her completely and obeys her about his back. *Juno:* teaches her a dovetail; she labels his tools. *The pie feud:* he said "ambitious" and meant it kindly.
**Tastes.** Loves wood, pumpkin, roasted roots (the bench lunch); likes stone, hazelnut, copper bar; dislikes dandelion, kale, slime.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and heart 5 exist. *Heart 8, "The Hall Roof":* he finally shows the player the roof plan; choices *help him measure*, *suggest a new design*, *offer to hold the ladder*.
**Quest.** *Planks for the Roof* (bring wood).
**Mythos note.** A Keeper (LORE.md). Cozy lines never hint at it; mythos lines live in `Farm.Mythos`.
**Samples that sound right.** "Measure twice." / "A straight board is a happy board." / "The roof is nearly fixed. Nearly is a respectable word."
**That do not.** "Quick, slap it together!" / A rant.

## 2. Dr. Odalys Penn (the village doctor)

**Role and place.** The clinic's doctor, with Elara as nurse. Closed Saturdays. Birthday Winter 3. Not romanceable.
**Public face.** Formal, brisk, precise; the person you are slightly afraid to disappoint.
**Real self.** Carries the village's health almost alone and never sleeps enough; privately takes her own advice only on Sundays.
**Voice rules.** Formal, complete sentences (up to 20 words), dry clinical metaphors, "Sit." as a greeting, no slang; at most one exclamation mark a line and rarely. Never cruel; her sharpness is concern.
**Humour type.** Deadpan clinical.
**Signature bit: *The prescription*.** She prescribes absurdly specific cozy remedies ("One hour of sunshine, a bowl of soup, and an unreasonable amount of cheese"). Catchphrase: **"Doctor's orders."** Sound: a soft stethoscope tap.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: a village that rests. Fears: being the one who could not help.
**Relationships.** *Elara:* a rivalry of warmth (the frown and the joke). *Marcus:* her most obedient patient. *Hazel, Ione:* she borrows medical history, returns it annotated. *Tilda:* the shop's herb shelf is hers by arrangement. *The player:* a patient she hopes never to see, and a friend she hopes to.
**Tastes.** Loves elderflower, spinach, salad; likes kale, snowdrop, cauliflower; dislikes slime, bone, clam.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and 5 exist. *Heart 8, "Office Hours":* a rare quiet evening; she takes her own prescription (soup, with the player); choices *insist she rests*, *share the soup*, *write her a prescription*.
**Quest.** *Elderflower for the Clinic* (bring elderflower).
**Mythos note.** The leader of the Keepers. In the cozy layer she is the careful, trusted village doctor and nothing else: no hint of nights, wood or watching. Mythos lines live in `Farm.Mythos`.
**Samples that sound right.** "Sit." / "Doctor's orders." / "Sleep is the best medicine and the cheapest."
**That do not.** "Sell me your pain." (horror leak) / "Whatever, you'll be fine." (slang)

## 3. Felix Hartwell (fish stall)

**Role and place.** Sells rods, bait and fish at the stall (06:00 to 14:00, closed Thursdays); fishes the beach and the forest pond. Birthday Summer 21. Romanceable.
**Public face.** Salty, slow, laconic; speaks in tide tables.
**Real self.** Has fished alone all his life and is quietly hoping someone will share the dawn. He tells stories so there is something to say.
**Voice rules.** Slow, plain, sea-and-bait metaphors, up to 18 words; "Mornin'." Mild exclamations only for a bite. Never mean about fish or fishers.
**Humour type.** The patient tall tale.
**Signature bit: *The one that got away*.** Every fish story grows by a hand each time ("It was this big. Last week it was this big."). Catchphrase: **"You should've seen the other one."** Sound: a bobber plop.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: a fishing partner; the sturgeon. Fears: a quiet dawn that stays quiet.
**Relationships.** *Tilda:* the oldest friendship; they bicker about fish prices (she sells none). *Bram:* repairs his hooks free and denies it. *Piper:* serenaded once; pretends not. *Wren:* sells her the saloon's fish and her best gossip. *The player:* "my dawn partner."
**Tastes.** Loves tuna, sturgeon and every fish; likes carp, seashell, sea bass; dislikes dandelion, kale, cucumber.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and 5 exist. *Heart 8, "The Big One":* a dawn at the pond; the player helps land a fish that grows with each telling; choices *net it*, *let it go*, *measure it honestly*.
**Quest.** *Bait for the Dawn* (bring bait).
**Mythos note.** Unaware; his cozy lines about the pond and the moon stay about fish.
**Samples that sound right.** "Mornin'." / "Patience is the whole trick. And bait." / "You should've seen the other one."
**That do not.** A modern slang joke; mockery of the player's fishing.

## 4. Dorian Lake (forager)

**Role and place.** Forages in the forest all day; lodges at the carpenter's. No shop. Birthday Winter 11. Romanceable.
**Public face.** Quiet, watchful, brief; politely elsewhere.
**Real self.** Shy rather than cold: prefers trees to crowds, is embarrassed by how long a sentence takes him, and keeps a notebook of tiny mushroom drawings that nobody has seen.
**Voice rules.** Very short sentences (up to 12 words), observation over opinion, no exclamation marks, nature words (root, moss, spore). Never explains himself; never mocks.
**Humour type.** Whispered forest literalism.
**Signature bit: *The offering*.** A small gift (a mushroom, a pinecone, a perfect leaf) appears on the player's step, and he denies it ("Not me. The forest."). Catchphrase: **"Not me. The forest."** Sound: a twig snap.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: a friend who does not need him to talk. Fears: being looked at.
**Relationships.** *Marcus:* the lodger; twelve words a week. *Hazel:* trades mushroom identifications for quiet. *Elara, Ione:* leave him alone, gently. *Bram:* nods; nothing more. *The player:* the only one who can sit in silence at the right length.
**Tastes.** Loves mushroom, truffle, wild garlic; likes hazelnut, blackberry, raspberry; dislikes tomato, pufferfish, slime.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and 5 exist. *Heart 8, "The Notebook":* he shows the player the mushroom drawings; choices *praise them*, *ask for one*, *draw one back*.
**Quest.** *A Basket for the Forest* (bring mushrooms).
**Mythos note.** A Keeper. Cozy lines stay about mushrooms, weather and quiet; "the forest is honest. Mostly." is the most the cozy layer says. Mythos lines live in `Farm.Mythos`.
**Samples that sound right.** "Quiet. You'll scare the mushrooms." / "Not me. The forest." / "Look under old roots. Everyone looks up."
**That do not.** A long speech; an exclamation mark.

## 5. Elara Finch (nurse)

**Role and place.** The clinic's nurse. Closed Saturdays (the beach). Birthday Spring 18. Romanceable.
**Public face.** Bright, bubbly, quick with a joke and a bandage.
**Real self.** Wants more than the clinic and keeps a list of places she has never been; knits when it is slow and hides the evidence from Dr. Penn.
**Voice rules.** Warm, bouncy, up to 20 words, gentle medical puns, exclamations welcome (one a line at most). Never makes fun of illness or injury; her jokes are about waiting rooms, tea and naps.
**Humour type.** Gentle silly medical puns.
**Signature bit: *The knitting*.** She knits increasingly absurd things (a sock for a chair, a hat for the clinic's plant) and gives them away. Catchphrase: **"Keep your socks on."** Sound: two needle clicks.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: to see the sea from a boat. Fears: staying so useful she never leaves.
**Relationships.** *Dr. Penn:* a warm rivalry (the joke and the frown). *Piper:* the first to hear a new song. *Juno:* knitted her a pair of fireproof-ish gloves. *Hazel:* books about far places. *The player:* "my favorite recovery."
**Tastes.** Loves snowdrop, cranberry, jam; likes seashell, spinach, juice; dislikes bone, bat wing, slime.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and 5 exist. *Heart 8, "The List":* she shows the player the list of places; choices *pick one together*, *add a place*, *promise to visit*.
**Quest.** *Wool for the Waiting Room* (bring wool).
**Mythos note.** Unaware.
**Samples that sound right.** "Keep your socks on." / "The best medicine is a good laugh. And rest. Mostly rest." / "I knit when it's slow. Don't tell Dr. Penn."
**That do not.** A joke at a patient's expense.

## 6. Ione Fairweather (librarian)

**Role and place.** Looks after the library; Hazel helps. Closed Saturdays (a walk in the forest). Birthday Winter 20. Romanceable.
**Public face.** Gentle, precise, quietly amused.
**Real self.** Quietly worried that the library has more stories than readers; loves the sea and the first snow and would never say so.
**Voice rules.** Gentle, exact, up to 20 words, bookish metaphors worn lightly, at most one exclamation mark a line. Never scolds; overdue is "a state of mind".
**Humour type.** Gentle librarian understatement.
**Signature bit: *The set-aside book*.** For every visitor she has quietly set a book aside, and it is always the right one. Catchphrase: **"I set something aside for you."** Sound: a small desk bell.
**Wants / fears.** Wants: more readers; the sea in winter. Fears: a library that stays a quiet room.
**Relationships.** *Hazel:* assistant, friend and keeper of the returns ledger. *Odalys:* swaps medical history for old almanacs. *Bram, Marcus:* both return books early without a word. *Wren:* borrows nothing, tells the library everything. *The player:* "the reader I hoped for."
**Tastes.** Loves pearl, snowdrop, elderflower; likes seashell, kale, blackberry; dislikes stone, wood, coal.
**Arc.** Heart 2 and 5 exist. *Heart 8, "The Set-Aside Shelf":* the player finds a shelf of books set aside for people who never came; choices *take one for someone*, *read one aloud*, *leave a note in one*.
**Quest.** *Pearls for the Reading Room* (bring a pearl).
**Mythos note.** Unaware.
**Samples that sound right.** "I set something aside for you." / "A good book is a quiet door." / "Overdue is a state of mind."
**That do not.** A scolding; shouting in a library.
