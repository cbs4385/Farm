STORYLINE: The Mystery Whistler (`mystery_whistler`)
STATUS: BUILT (approved 2026-10-03). The sections below keep the original proposal for reference; the shipped lines and scene are in Assets/_Project/Narrative/storyline_*.fscript and Resources/Story/storyline_events.json.
Tone: cozy mystery. At dusk someone whistles the same tune near the village and nobody is seen.

## Premise
A cheerful tune with one wrong note. Ione hears it from the clinic, Juno from the quarry road. The whistler is harmless: a villager who thinks they only whistle when alone.

## Beats (proposed)
1. Dusk talk lines for three villagers (the tune, where they heard it).
2. The player is asked to listen: a dusk scene on the road with a sound cue and no one on screen.
3. Reveal (who is chosen from a pool, so each game differs): the baker, the blacksmith (Bram, mortified) or the carpenter.
4. Payoff: the wrong note becomes a village joke; someone hums it at a festival.

## Sample lines (draft)
- Ione: "A tune with one wrong note. It lives in my head now. Thank you, whoever you are."
- Juno: "It sounds like a kettle trying to be a flute."
- Bram (if he is the whistler): "Hm. I whistle. Don't."

## Needs and caution
- Audio: a short tune cue. It fits the procedural audio style or needs the composer.
- It is a cozy mystery on purpose: it must not hint at the horror layer.
