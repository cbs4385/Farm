STORYLINE: The Pie Feud (`pie_feud`)
STATUS: BUILT (approved 2026-10-03). The sections below keep the original proposal for reference; the shipped lines and scene are in Assets/_Project/Narrative/storyline_*.fscript and Resources/Story/storyline_events.json.
Tone: funny. Stakes: tiny. Marcus and Odalys are not speaking over a pie. Wren runs a betting pool, Hazel files it, Bram would fix it with a hammer.

## Premise
Someone called the other's pie crust "ambitious". Nine days later the pie is still on a windowsill and nobody dares touch it.

## Lines in the game now (priority 2, only when this storyline is drawn)
- Wren (`wren.pie.1`): "Marcus and Odalys are not speaking over a pie. I'm running a betting pool. Don't tell them."
- Wren (`wren.pie.2`): "The pie feud's day nine. The pie is still on the windowsill. Nobody dares touch it."
- Hazel (`hazel.pie.1`): "A pie, two neighbors. (I'm cataloguing the feud under "Domestic.") Nobody asked."
- Hazel (`hazel.pie.2`): "The pie has gone stale. (The feud hasn't.) I find that instructive."
- Bram (`bram.pie.1`): "Hm. Pie feud. Two fools, one crust. I'd fix it with a hammer."
- Bram (`bram.pie.2`): "Hm. Both sides asked me to make a pie plate. I made one. Gave it to Juno."

## PROPOSED additions (not in the game)
- Marcus: "I said ambitious. It was a compliment. She heard what she wanted to hear."
- Odalys: "Ambitious is what you call a house with no roof. I have thoughts, [player]. Sit."
- Beat 1: the player hears both sides over a few days in the village.
- Payoff scene (3 hearts with either): the player is handed one slice to carry to the other. Choices: deliver it honestly / "improve" it / eat it. Result: the feud ends, goes on, or the player becomes the third party. Sets `storyline.pie_feud.done`.
- After: Wren pays out the bet, or says the odds were wrong.

## Risks
- Marcus and Odalys are not full-depth villagers yet; the payoff needs both in voice.
- Keep the joke on the situation, never on a villager's body or class.
