STORYLINE: The Rival Scarecrows (`rival_scarecrows`)
STATUS: BUILT (approved 2026-10-03). The sections below keep the original proposal for reference; the shipped lines and scene are in Assets/_Project/Narrative/storyline_*.fscript and Resources/Story/storyline_events.json.
Tone: competitive, silly. Two villagers each built a scarecrow and each claims theirs scares crows better.

## Premise
Tilda and Dorian enter a scarecrow contest in the fall, and the player is asked to judge, which satisfies nobody.

## Beats (proposed)
1. Summer: Tilda mentions the contest. Dorian's scarecrow is wearing his hat.
2. Fall: both scarecrows stand in the village square; the player visits each.
3. Judgment (choice): Tilda's / Dorian's / a tie / "the crows decide". Reaction lines for both and for Wren (who bets) and Bram.
4. Aftermath: the winner's scarecrow appears on the player's farm as a decoration, or a ribbon item.

## Sample lines (draft)
- Tilda: "Mine has a smile. Smiles scare crows. That is simply known."
- Dorian: "Mine is quiet. Quiet is unsettling. I did my research."

## Needs
- A scene in the village square and an optional decoration item. An owner decision is needed on whether it touches the festivals (a binding scope area).
