STORYLINE: The Lost Umbrella (`lost_umbrella`)
STATUS: PROPOSAL. Nothing in the game except the storyline entry.
Tone: gentle comedy. A large red umbrella goes missing in the first rain of the year and turns up somewhere improbable.

## Premise
Piper lost it, Felix swears he returned it, Elara says it was never hers. The umbrella reappears on rainy days in a new place.

## Beats (proposed)
1. First rain: Piper asks around. Talk lines for Piper, Felix, Elara (2 each).
2. A second sighting on the player's farm (a prop in the scene on a rainy day).
3. Choice: return it to Piper, hang it where it was found, or keep it. Each gives a different warm reply.
4. A closing line from the one who "never had it".

## Sample lines (draft)
- Piper: "It's red. It's enormous. It can't simply vanish, and yet."
- Felix: "I returned it. I remember because it dripped on my best boots."
- Elara: "An umbrella. If it were mine, I would have named it."

## Needs
- A rain-only prop and a weather condition. The weather conditions exist, so no new engine work.
