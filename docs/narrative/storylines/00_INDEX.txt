Status: APPROVED by the owner (2026-10-03) and BUILT: all five have setup lines, a payoff scene with choices, and after-the-scene lines. Not yet read in play by a person.

## How storylines work (for the reviewer)
- Each new game draws 4 of the 8 storylines from its own seed. The draw is stored as the flag `storyline.<id>` and never changes in that game. Two games differ; the same game always plays the same.
- A storyline is made of ordinary lines that only play when the game has drawn it. It never blocks anything: a game without it is complete.
- Storyline lines sit at dialogue priority 2: above everyday chat, below reactions, quests and scenes.
- All cozy. No horror content (the mythos layer is separate).
