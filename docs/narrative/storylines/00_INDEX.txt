Status: DRAFT by the agent. Not read by a person. Nothing here is approved.

## How storylines work (for the reviewer)
- Each new game draws 4 of the 5 storylines from its own seed. The draw is stored as the flag `storyline.<id>` and never changes in that game. Two games differ; the same game always plays the same.
- A storyline is made of ordinary lines that only play when the game has drawn it. It never blocks anything: a game without it is complete.
- Storyline lines sit at dialogue priority 2: above everyday chat, below reactions, quests and scenes.
- All cozy. No horror content (the mythos layer is separate).

## What to decide
1. Do the two live storylines read as funny, warm and in-voice? Mark lines to keep, rewrite or cut.
2. Are the three proposals worth building? Choose, reorder or replace them.
3. Should each storyline have a small payoff scene (a heart-event-sized beat)? The proposals suggest one each.
4. Is 4 of 5 the right draw, or should a game get 3 of 5 so two players more often differ?
5. Should a storyline be mentioned in a journal or stay a pleasant surprise? (Recommendation: stay a surprise.)
