## Done

### 1. One occupant per tile, solid and routed around
- `CellOccupants` (`Gameplay/World/CellOccupants.cs`) tracks every villager, the village cat and every farm animal, and the cell each is walking into. Villagers follow their schedule and never give way; the cat and the animals never choose a held cell and step aside when somebody arrives on theirs.
- Villagers, the cat and animals have a solid body (`ActorBody`), so the player cannot walk through them and nothing can be set down on them. The route grid ignores those bodies (people are not walls).
- `WalkGrid.FindPath` takes an optional `occupied` predicate. A villager's walk, the cat's routes and the animals' steps go around occupied cells; a villager with no way around falls back to the plain route so nobody is ever stuck.
- Three pairs of villagers shared a stop in the schedule data (Bram and Marcus, Felix and Tilda, Hazel and Ione); fixed, with a test over all seasons, weekdays and weather that fails if any two villagers ever stand on one tile.
- Known gap: two villagers crossing in mid-walk can briefly share a tile; the player is not part of the rule.

### 4. Larger counters, bin and reach
- Shop counters, bars, desks and pack counters are now 2 tiles wide (`obj_counter_wide`, 32x16 art made with `generate_sheet.py wide`); the shipping bin is 2x2 (`obj_shipping_bin`, 32x32). Their colliders cover the whole footprint.
- `IInteractable.Reach`: counters, the bin and the library desk can be used from one tile further away (the cell beyond the one in front of the player).
- Layout shifts to keep the schedules valid: Ione's library stop and the player cell of her scenes moved one tile east. `NpcSceneTests` now blocks every cell a collider covers.
- Open question for the owner: whether this is the size the tester meant, or whether the counters should also be taller or deeper. The stall (`obj_stall`) was not changed.

### 5. A clicked villager stops
- Talking to a villager (or clicking where they are walking, now matched within 0.75 tile) holds them for 12 seconds of real time that only runs while no dialogue is open (`NpcManager.Hold`). While held they fall behind the schedule at the clock rate; afterwards they walk at twice the speed until they have caught up (`NpcManager.NextLag`), so nobody jumps.

### 6. Conversations keep going
- After a chat every menu now offers "Keep chatting" (another line from the same villager, with the menu back afterwards) next to Goodbye, even when there are no topics or social actions left (`InteractionMenu.WithMore`, `Minimal`). Goodbye stays the default, so a habitual Enter still ends the chat. Talking more does not give extra friendship.
- Not addressed: clicking twice per line (the first click finishes the typing, the second moves on) is how the dialogue box has always worked.

## How it was built (phases B to D)
- `FarmBuildings` (types, default places, placement rules), `FarmBuildingState` list in `GameState.FarmBuildings` (an old save is seeded with the default places), `FarmBuildingsView` (draws walls, roof, door and the door warp and return spawn at run time; `MapBuilder` no longer bakes them; the fixed route edges to the three interiors were removed from `MapRoutes`).
- **Builder's Mallet** (`tool.hammer`, a new tool type; every new game starts with one, older saves get one once through the backpack or the mailbox). `HammerMode` on the player: swing at a building (farm), a fixture (the bed and the kitchen, `MovableFixture`, saved in `GameState.Fixtures`; the bed's wake-up spawn goes with it) or anything set down (`ObjectGrid.Move`, chests keep their contents); a green or red ghost follows the target square; swing again to put it down; the Interact button puts it back. Lifted things stay in the saved state at their old place until put down.
- Rules: a building needs open grass or dirt (no tree, rock, plant, dug soil, object or water), a free cell in front of the door, a gap of one cell to other buildings, and not on or touching the farmhouse (which never moves). Furniture may not block a doorway.
- Open for the owner: whether buildings should be able to be put in the farmhouse yard only, whether moving should cost anything, whether the shipping bin and the mailbox should also be movable (not done), a pre-placed hint for new players (the mallet has no tutorial yet).
