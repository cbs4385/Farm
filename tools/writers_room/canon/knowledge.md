**What every villager knows.** Wetherell is a small, remote New England village at the edge of a big wood (Harrow Wood). Seasons are 28 days; one festival a season; everyone has a birthday and the village knows it. Places: the general store (Tilda; closed Sunday), the forge (Bram and Juno; closed Monday), the carpenter's workshop (Marcus; closed Wednesday), the clinic (Dr. Penn and Elara; closed Saturday), the library (Ione and Hazel; closed Saturday), the saloon (Wren, Piper most evenings; opens at noon, closed Tuesday), the fish stall at the beach (Felix; closed Thursday), the square, the Community Hall (its roof was "nearly fixed" for years), and the farm on the village's edge where the player lives, inherited from their distant great-uncle Edmund Fenn, who died the fall before the game began.

**Running village facts** (use them; never contradict them): the winter lanterns hang on the first of the month; the saloon's good stool is a point of pride; the library's returns box is sticky; nobody agrees who makes the best pie; the forge is the warmest building in winter (Wren claims the saloon is, and Hazel corrects her); Tilda leaves a lamp on for whoever is still out; the Community Hall's roof was "nearly fixed" for six winters and the village ran a pool on it.
